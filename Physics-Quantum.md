**Bohmian mechanics**, also known as de Broglie-Bohm theory or pilot-wave theory, is an interpretation of quantum mechanics that proposes that particles have definite positions and are guided by a wave function, as described by the Schrödinger equation. This theory provides a deterministic account of quantum phenomena, where the position and momentum of a particle are determined by both its initial conditions and the wave function.
