> What is Proper Time?

Proper time (\(\tau \)) is the time interval between two events measured by a single clock present at both events, 
representing the actual time experienced along a specific trajectory. It is an invariant quantity in relativity, 
meaning all observers agree on the elapsed proper time, **unlike coordinate time**.
